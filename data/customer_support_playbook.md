# Customer Support Playbook

Owner: Customer Support Operations. Last reviewed: 2026-06-01.

## Support hours

Live support runs 09:00-18:00 (Asia/Dhaka), Sunday to Thursday. P1 incidents are covered
24/7 by the on-call engineer rota.

## Ticket priorities and SLAs

| Priority | Meaning                              | First response   | Resolution target    |
|----------|--------------------------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| P1       | Production down, no workaround       | 15 minutes       | 4 hours              |
| P2       | Major feature broken, workaround     | 1 hour           | 1 business day       |
| P3       | Minor issue or partial degradation   | 4 business hours | 3 business days     |
| P4       | Question or feature request          | 1 business day   | 10 business days    |

For a P1, page the on-call engineer immediately and notify the customer's Customer
Success Manager. If a P1 has no engineer acknowledgement after 30 minutes, escalate to
the Head of Engineering.

## Escalation path

Tickets start at Tier 1. Escalate to Tier 2 when there is no progress after 2 hours or
when the issue needs log access. Tier 2 escalates to the product engineering team only
with a reproducible case attached.

## Refunds and credits

Tier 2 leads can approve refunds or service credits up to USD 200. Anything above
USD 200 must be approved by the Support Director, Farhana Rahman.

## Closing tickets

When a ticket is waiting on the customer, send reminders on day 3 and day 6. The ticket
auto-closes after 7 days of customer inactivity. A closed ticket can be reopened within
14 days by replying to the last email.

## Quality targets

Our CSAT target is 4.5 out of 5. Every agent has 5 tickets per week reviewed by a team
lead against the quality rubric.
