# HR Leave Policy

Policy HR-04, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all full-time employees.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at
1.67 days per month. Employees on probation (the first 6 months) accrue leave but may
take at most 3 days of it before probation ends.

Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over
days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they expire. At year end, employees may instead
encash up to 10 unused days at their basic salary rate.

Annual leave longer than 3 days must be requested at least 5 working days in advance.
Managers must approve or reject a request within 2 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried over
or encashed.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 10 days of casual leave per year, with a maximum of 3 consecutive days.
Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave.

## Parental leave

Maternity leave is 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of
paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Leave without pay

Leave without pay may be granted for up to 30 days per year. It requires approval from
the HR Director, currently Tanvir Hossain, in addition to the line manager.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the government calendar published every December. If a public
holiday falls inside approved annual leave, that day is not deducted from the balance.
